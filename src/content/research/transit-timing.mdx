---
title: transit timing variations in compact multi-planet systems
summary: measuring how planets tug on each other by watching their transits drift
date: 2026-03-20
authors: [sanyukta shubha]
venue: undergraduate research symposium
---

When two planets orbit close together, each one arrives at its transit a little
early or a little late depending on where the other one is. Those small drifts are
enough to weigh the planets without ever seeing them directly.
