You can't fix what you don't feel. This puts you through side-by-side simulations
of what a page is like with low vision, motion sensitivity, or cognitive load,
complete with pop-ups, flashing ads, keyboard traps, and jargon floods. You'll hate
it. That's the point.

Each one ends with the actual wcag-based fix built in, and the whole thing ends with
a fake but real-looking certificate you can share.

React and vite, framer motion for the chaos, plain css.
