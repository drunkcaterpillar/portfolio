A content script that sits on top of x, blurs posts that read as bait or hostility,
and injects small cards that make the civil version of the opposing argument on
whatever topic you're scrolling. The idea is a nudge toward empathy rather than a
filter bubble in the other direction.

A prototype, and honest about it: the catalog of balanced cards is hand-written and
tiny. The interesting question was never the plumbing, it was whether a feed can be
made calmer without deciding for you what's true.
