---
title: exoplanet explorer
summary: a small web tool that turns kepler light curves into a list of transit candidates
date: 2026-05-14
tags: [python, astronomy]
---

Stars dim a tiny bit when a planet passes in front of them. This takes a raw light
curve, flattens out the star's own wobble, and looks for dips that repeat on a
steady clock.

The interesting part was deciding what counts as a repeat. Real transits are boring
and regular. Noise is not, but it is very good at pretending.
