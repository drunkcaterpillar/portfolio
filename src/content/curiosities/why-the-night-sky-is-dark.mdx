---
title: why the night sky is dark
summary: olbers' paradox, and the answer that took a few centuries to arrive
date: 2026-02-27
tags: [astronomy, rabbit-hole]
---

If the universe were infinite, eternal, and full of stars, every line of sight
would end on a star and the night sky would be as bright as the sun. It is not.
The resolution is that the universe has an age, and light has a speed, and
together those put a horizon on how much sky can be lit.
